# PI_E_002: Mass–radius relation of a white dwarf

*Paraphrased from USAPhO 2024, A2 c (official solution by the AAPT committee).*

## statement

A white dwarf of mass `M` and radius `R` has a roughly uniform density
`ρ ~ M/R³`. Its pressure is supplied by non-relativistic degenerate
electrons, `P = Kρ^{5/3}`. Assuming its central pressure depends only on
`G`, `M`, `R`, show that `R ∝ Mⁿ` and find `n`.

## proof

**[Physical decision]** The only pressure that can be built from `G`
(`L³M⁻¹T⁻²`), `M` and `R` is `P ~ GM²/R⁴`; no other length enters because
the density is uniform. Equating this with `K(M/R³)^{5/3} ∝ M^{5/3}/R⁵` gives
`R ∝ M^{-1/3}`. More massive white dwarfs are smaller.

(The degeneracy exponent comes from `Δp ~ ħ n^{1/3}`,
`P ~ n·Δp·Δv ∝ n^{5/3}`. The Lean statement takes it as given.)
