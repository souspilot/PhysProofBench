# CAN_00_002_001 — A spin squared is one

Canary item: a trivial pipeline sanity check, not part of the
benchmark (`docs/SOURCE.md#can`). Statement and proof written by the
contributor.

## statement

A spin takes the value `+1` or `-1`; its square is `1`.

## proof

Check both values: `1 · 1 = 1` and `(-1) · (-1) = 1`.
