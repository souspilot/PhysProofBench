# CAN_00_004_001 — Curie–Weiss partition function is positive

Canary item: a trivial pipeline sanity check, not part of the
benchmark (`docs/SOURCE.md#can`). Statement and proof written by the
contributor.

## statement

The Curie–Weiss partition function, a sum of Boltzmann weights `e^{-H(ω)}` over all spin configurations, is strictly positive.

## proof

Every exponential is positive, and there is at least one configuration, so the sum is positive.
